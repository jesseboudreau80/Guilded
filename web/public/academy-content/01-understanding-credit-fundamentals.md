## Lesson 1.1: Anatomy of a Credit Score

### What is a Credit Score?

Definition and purpose of credit scores.

How lenders use credit scores to evaluate risk.

Examples of when your credit score matters (e.g., loans, housing, employment).

### The Factors Behind Your Credit Score

Payment History (35%): The impact of missed payments and delinquencies.

Credit Utilization (30%): Why keeping balances low is key.

Credit History Length (15%): Why time plays a critical role.

Credit Mix (10%): The benefits of having a variety of credit accounts.

New Credit (10%): How inquiries and new accounts affect your score.

### Understanding the Scoring Models

FICO Score vs. VantageScore: Similarities and differences.

Industry-specific credit scores (e.g., mortgage, auto loan scores).

## Lesson 1.2: What is a Credit Report?

### Breaking Down a Credit Report

Sections of a credit report (Personal Information, Accounts, Inquiries, Public Records).

Common inaccuracies to look for (misspelled names, incorrect balances, duplicate accounts).

### Where to Access Your Credit Report

Free annual reports through AnnualCreditReport.com.

Additional monitoring services and tools (e.g., Credit Karma, Experian).

### How to Interpret Your Credit Report

Identifying positive and negative factors.

Assessing patterns that affect your credit health.

Recognizing accounts with errors that may need to be disputed.

## Lesson 1.3: The Importance of Credit in Financial Health

### How Credit Impacts Your Life

Real-life scenarios where credit scores matter (renting an apartment, securing a loan, getting lower interest rates).

The long-term benefits of good credit health.

### The Consequences of Bad Credit

Higher interest rates and fees.

Difficulty securing credit or housing.

Increased financial stress.

### Building a Credit Repair Mindset

Adopting the Guild's motto: "Preparation is the key to victory."

Understanding that credit repair is a long-term process requiring patience and strategy.
