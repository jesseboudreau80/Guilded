This module focuses on the active steps of credit repair, providing participants with actionable strategies to dispute negative items, protect their rights, and clean up their credit report. Through detailed templates, examples, and Guild-themed tools, participants will learn how to take charge of their credit repair journey.

## Lesson 4.1: Identifying Negative Accounts

### What Are Negative Accounts?

Late payments, charge-offs, collections, and bankruptcies.

How these items impact your credit score and financial opportunities.

### Assessing Negative Items

Reviewing the "Accounts" and "Public Records" sections of your credit report.

Differentiating between valid, invalid, and unverifiable accounts.

### Prioritizing Disputes

Targeting high-impact items first (e.g., recent late payments vs. older ones).

Using the Guild Dispute Priority List to rank items by importance.

## Lesson 4.2: Crafting Effective Dispute Strategies

### The Guild Dispute Philosophy

"Attack the weak points first, and the strong will crumble."

Why challenging inaccuracies in personal information first weakens the foundation of negative accounts.

### Step-by-Step Dispute Process

Identifying inaccurate or unverifiable items.

Writing Attack Letters to dispute with credit bureaus.

Following up with creditors directly when necessary.

### Templates for Every Scenario

"Precision Archer Letter": For disputing late payments and small errors.

"Siege Engine Letter": For collections, charge-offs, and larger disputes.

"Follow-Up Warrior Letter": For challenging unresponsive bureaus or creditors.

## Lesson 4.3: Understanding the Dispute Timeline

### Key Deadlines

30--45 days for credit bureaus to investigate disputes.

Timelines for follow-ups if no response is received.

### Tracking Progress

Using the Guild Dispute Journal to log disputes, dates, and responses.

Monitoring changes to your credit report after disputes.

## Lesson 4.4: Challenging Collection Accounts

### Debt Validation

How to request debt validation from collection agencies under the FDCPA.

Templates for "Debt Validation Defense Letters."

### Pay-for-Delete Strategies

Negotiating with collection agencies to remove accounts upon payment.

How to structure "Barterman's Agreement Letters."

### Disputing Collections Directly with Bureaus

Steps to challenge unverifiable or inaccurate collection accounts.

Real-world examples of successful collection disputes.

## Lesson 4.5: Tackling Charge-Offs and Public Records

### What Are Charge-Offs?

Understanding how charge-offs occur and their impact on your credit.

Strategies to dispute or settle charge-offs.

### Handling Public Records

Bankruptcy, tax liens, and judgments: What you can and cannot dispute.

Steps to ensure public records are reported accurately.

### Guild Templates for Advanced Disputes

"Warrior's Tax Lien Dispute Letter": For tax liens and judgments.

"Bankruptcy Shield Letter": For ensuring accurate reporting post-bankruptcy.

## Lesson 4.6: Protecting Your Rights During Disputes

### Common Creditor and Bureau Tactics

Ignoring disputes or failing to provide adequate proof.

Reporting previously removed items.

### How to Respond

Writing "Follow-Up Warrior Letters" to hold bureaus accountable.

Filing complaints with the CFPB and FTC for unresolved issues.

### Escalating to Arbitration

When and how to escalate disputes to arbitration if necessary.

Using arbitration as a negotiation tool against creditors or bureaus.
