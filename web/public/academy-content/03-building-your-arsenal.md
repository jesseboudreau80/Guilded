This module is designed to equip participants with the tools, techniques, and strategies to rebuild and strengthen their credit. It focuses on budgeting, improving credit utilization, using credit cards wisely, and understanding the impact of inquiries. Participants will leave this module with actionable strategies to establish a strong foundation for credit rebuilding.

## Lesson 3.1: Effective Budgeting Techniques

### Why Budgeting is Critical

Budgeting as the backbone of financial stability and credit rebuilding.

The Guild mantra: "A disciplined knight always accounts for their resources."

### Creating a Guild-Worthy Budget

Step-by-step guide to building a zero-based budget.

Using the "50/30/20 Rule" to allocate income.

Identifying and reducing unnecessary expenses.

### Tools and Resources

The Guild Budget Tracker: A themed spreadsheet to track income, expenses, and savings.

Example scenarios for budgeting in various income brackets.

## Lesson 3.2: Improving Credit Utilization

### What is Credit Utilization?

Definition and why it accounts for 30% of your credit score.

Ideal utilization rates and how they impact your score.

### Strategies for Reducing Utilization

Paying down high balances systematically (Avalanche vs. Snowball methods).

Timing credit card payments to optimize reported balances.

Requesting credit limit increases (when and how to do it safely).

### Guild Tools

The Credit Utilization Shield: A calculator to help manage balances and target optimal utilization rates.

Step-by-step walkthrough of balancing credit limits.

## Lesson 3.3: Secured vs. Unsecured Credit Cards

### Understanding Secured Credit Cards

How secured cards work and their role in credit rebuilding.

Selecting the best secured cards (low fees, reporting to all bureaus).

### Transitioning to Unsecured Cards

When and how to graduate to unsecured credit cards.

Identifying red flags in predatory credit card offers.

### The Guild Credit Card Mastery Guide

A visual comparison of secured vs. unsecured cards.

Tips for using credit cards responsibly to avoid future debt traps.

## Lesson 3.4: The Impact of Credit Inquiries

### Hard vs. Soft Inquiries

Definitions and examples of each.

How hard inquiries affect your credit score.

### Managing Inquiries

Strategies to limit unnecessary hard inquiries.

How to dispute unauthorized inquiries using "Precision Archer Letters."

### Guild Inquiry Tracker

A tool to log and monitor credit inquiries.

Templates for challenging unauthorized hard pulls.

## Lesson 3.5: Establishing New Credit Lines

### When to Open New Accounts

Timing new credit lines to optimize your credit score.

Choosing the right types of credit (credit cards, loans, etc.).

### Building a Diverse Credit Mix

How a mix of installment loans and revolving credit can improve your score.

Common mistakes to avoid when opening new accounts.

### Guild Tools

Sample scripts for contacting creditors or banks.
