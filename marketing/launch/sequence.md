## Post frequency guidelines

- Maximum 3–4 posts per week in the group
- Never post two product-forward posts back to back — alternate educational/product
- If a post isn't getting traction, don't boost immediately — wait 48 hours, consider whether the educational value was clear

## The rule that overrides everything

**Educational first. Product second.**
If every post teaches something true and useful — whether or not they ever sign up — the community trusts you.
That trust is the conversion mechanism.
